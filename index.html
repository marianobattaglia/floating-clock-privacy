Privacy Policy for FloatingClock

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Developer: man0bat
Privacy contact: marianobattaglia.dev@gmail.com

Summary

FloatingClock is an Android TV app that displays a floating clock and lets you set an alarm. The app does not require an account or sign-in and does not include advertising, analytics, or Firebase.

Information stored by the app

The app stores settings needed for its features, such as clock preferences, appearance, alarm settings, and PRO access status. This information is stored locally in the app's private storage on your device and is not sent to a developer-operated server. It is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Google Play may retain purchase records in your Google Account under its own policies.

The app does not ask for or collect your name, email address, or user ID to create an account.

Google Play purchases

The app offers an optional one-time purchase to unlock PRO through Google Play Billing. Google Play processes the purchase. The app checks purchase status with Google Play and may handle purchase information temporarily to acknowledge a completed transaction. The app does not retain the purchase token or send it to a developer-operated server. It stores the PRO access status locally on your device. Payment details are processed by Google Play and are not stored by the developer.

Google's handling of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Device permissions

FloatingClock may request Android permissions needed to display the clock over other apps, schedule alarms, show notifications, and play the alarm sound. These permissions support features you choose to use and are not used to collect personal information.

Sharing

The developer does not operate a server that receives FloatingClock usage data and does not sell or share personal information. Google Play processes the information needed for purchases under its own policies.

Security and retention

FloatingClock settings remain in the app's private storage on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. A PRO purchase is associated with the Google Play account used to buy it and can be restored through Google Play.

Children's privacy

FloatingClock does not request personal information or create user profiles. Any purchases are handled through Google Play and are subject to its controls and requirements.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The current version and its update date will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, email marianobattaglia.dev@gmail.com.
